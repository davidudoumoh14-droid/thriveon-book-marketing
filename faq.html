Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about the 90-day roadmap, packages, support, and how we work with authors.

What exactly do I receive?

A custom written 90-day book marketing roadmap built around your book, audience, and goals, plus guided email support while you execute it. Depending on the package you also receive templates, checklists, progress reviews, resource library access, and in higher tiers deeper strategy documents (messaging guide, multi-channel plan, content calendar, sales funnel outline, etc.).

Are there live one-on-one consultation calls?

No. All support is provided via email. There are no live one-on-one consultation calls in any package. This keeps the offer focused, affordable, and scalable while still giving you clear guidance and accountability.

How is this different from a course or a generic checklist?

The roadmap is built for your specific book and situation after we learn about your project. You also get ongoing email support and scheduled progress reviews — not a static PDF you have to interpret alone. The goal is a plan that gets executed, not more information sitting unused.

Do you run my ads or manage my social media for me?

No. This is a guided strategy and accountability product. We design the plan and support you while you implement it. We do not run Amazon/BookBub ads accounts or manage social media profiles as part of these packages. If you need heavy done-for-you execution later, we can discuss that separately.

How long does it take to receive the roadmap?

Delivery depends on the package:

Basic: within 7–10 days
Advanced: within 5–7 days
Premium: within 3–5 days

Timing starts after we have the information we need about your book and goals.

What is the difference between Basic, Advanced, and Premium?

Basic ($899) gives you a solid custom 90-day roadmap, core milestones, templates, email support, two progress reviews, and standard resource library access.

Advanced ($1,899) adds an expanded multi-channel plan, audience positioning & messaging guide, priority email support, three progress reviews, bonus resource pack, launch/relaunch sequence outline, and full library access.

Premium ($2,699) includes everything in Advanced plus a fully customized 90-day + 30-day extension roadmap, complete content & promotion calendar, sales funnel & offer strategy outline, highest priority support, four progress reviews, premium resource vault, and a post-roadmap 30-day action plan.

See the Pricing page for the full feature lists.

Who is this best suited for?

Independent and hybrid authors who are ready to treat marketing as a real system — whether you’re preparing to launch, relaunching a title, or building momentum on existing books. It works especially well if you want structure and accountability without the cost or schedule pressure of ongoing live coaching.

What happens after I submit my book?

We review your submission and follow up within 2–3 business days. We’ll confirm fit, answer questions, and help you choose the right package if you’re unsure. Once you’re ready to move forward and we’ve collected the details we need, we build and deliver your roadmap on the timeline for your package.

Is support available after the 90 days?

The formal guided support period matches your package (90 days, with Premium including a 30-day extension roadmap). You keep all materials and systems. If you want continued support afterward, we can discuss options separately.

How do I get started?

Use the Submit Your Book form. Tell us about your project, goals, and preferred package. We’ll take it from there.
