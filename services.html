Your Custom 90-Day Roadmap

We design a clear, actionable 90-day plan for your book and guide you through the entire process — so you move from overwhelm to consistent progress and real results.

What we actually do for you

We don’t hand you a generic checklist and disappear. We build a personalized plan and stay with you while you execute it.

Step 1

We build the roadmap

After learning about your book, audience, current platform, and goals, we produce a written 90-day plan. It prioritizes the highest-impact actions for your specific situation — not every possible tactic under the sun.

You’ll know exactly what to focus on first, what can wait, and how each piece connects to measurable progress.

  • Positioning & ideal-reader clarity
  • Prioritized milestones for the full 90 days
  • Channel and tactic recommendations matched to your book
  • Clear success metrics so you can track what works
Step 2

We guide you through it

You are not left alone with a PDF. Throughout the 90 days you receive structured email support: answers to questions, help adjusting when something isn’t landing, and accountability so the plan turns into real action.

There are no live one-on-one consultation calls. Support is practical, written, and designed so you can move at a sustainable pace.

  • Ongoing email guidance during the roadmap
  • Scheduled progress reviews (number depends on package)
  • Templates, checklists, and resource access
  • Adjustments based on what’s actually working
Step 3

You leave with results and systems

By the end of the 90 days the goal is twofold: measurable movement (visibility, engagement, list growth, or sales-related metrics) and systems you can keep running without starting from zero every month.

Higher packages add deeper strategy, more resources, faster support, and in Premium a 30-day extension roadmap so momentum doesn’t stop at day 90.

  • Documented progress and clear next steps
  • Reusable templates and processes
  • Stronger positioning and messaging
  • Confidence to continue growing independently
Important

What this is — and isn’t

This is: a guided strategy and accountability product. We design the plan and support you while you implement it via email.

This is not: a done-for-you ads management service, full social media management, or live coaching calls. If you need heavy execution done for you, we can discuss that separately after the roadmap.

Every package is delivered digitally. Support is email-only. There are no live one-on-one consultation calls in any package.

The 90-day journey in detail

A clear structure so you always know what to focus on next.

Phase 1 · Days 1–15

Foundation & Clarity

We clarify your positioning, ideal reader, and core message. You get an honest picture of where you stand and which levers will move the needle fastest. The custom roadmap is finalized with priorities and milestones so the rest of the 90 days has a solid base.

Phase 2 · Days 16–45

Launch the Core Strategy

You implement the highest-impact marketing and audience-building actions. We guide you on content, outreach, platforms, and offers so momentum builds quickly. Templates and checklists keep the work concrete and doable instead of overwhelming.

Phase 3 · Days 46–75

Optimize & Scale

We review what’s working, refine your approach, and expand the tactics that deliver results. You start seeing clearer traction, more consistent engagement, and stronger systems around your book and author presence.

Phase 4 · Days 76–90

Systems & Next Level

We lock in the systems that keep working without constant reinvention. You leave with measurable progress, a clear path forward, and the confidence to continue growing your book’s reach and impact on your own.

Who this is for

Authors ready to market seriously

Writers who want accountability

You know marketing matters but struggle to stay consistent. A clear roadmap plus ongoing email guidance and progress reviews keeps you moving without needing live calls.

Authors seeking real results

You want practical strategies that lead to measurable growth — more readers, stronger presence, better systems, and lasting impact for your work.
