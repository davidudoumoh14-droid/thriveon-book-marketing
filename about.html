About Thriveon

We exist to help authors move from “I published a book” to “readers are finding and loving my work.”

Marketing that respects the craft

Writing a book is hard. Marketing one shouldn’t feel impossible or dishonest. Thriveon was created for authors who want professional support without the hype, pressure tactics, or one-size-fits-all packages that ignore genre and goals.

We focus on strategy first: understanding your book, your audience, and where you are in your career. Then we apply the right mix of paid advertising, organic content, email growth, and launch planning so every effort compounds.

Whether you’re preparing for your first launch or looking to grow an existing series, we meet you where you are and build a clear path forward.

Our mission

Help authors build sustainable readership and careers through honest, effective book marketing.

Who we work with

Independent and hybrid authors across fiction and non-fiction who are ready to invest in growth and treat their writing as a serious creative business.

Clarity over noise

We prioritize strategies that make sense for your book and budget, not every shiny new tactic.

Partnership mindset

You’re the expert on your story. We’re the experts on getting it in front of the right readers.

Measurable progress

We track what matters — visibility, engagement, sales, and list growth — and adjust based on real data.

Respect for the work

Marketing should serve the book and the author, never cheapen the craft or mislead readers.
